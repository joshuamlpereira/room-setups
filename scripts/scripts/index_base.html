The setups

Each photo is tagged with the room it was taken in. Most setups can be built in any room as long as the group fits.

Banquet

Round tables with chairs all the way around. Each round seats up to 8.

Good for Dinners, receptions, award nights, group work.

Lecture

Rows of chairs facing the front. No tables.

Good for Talks, presentations, info sessions.

Panel

Rows of chairs facing a long head table for the speakers.

Good for Panel talks, Q and A sessions, hearings.

Job Fair

Long tables around the edge of the room with the middle left open for walking.

Good for Job fairs, vendor tables, check in, info fairs.

Classroom

Rows of long tables with chairs on one side, all facing the front. In a V shape the rows are angled toward the middle so everyone can see the front.

Good for Training, testing, note taking, workshops.

Conference

Long tables pushed together in a rectangle with chairs around the outside.

Good for Meetings where everyone needs to see each other.

Square

Long tables set in a large open square with chairs around the outside.

Good for Bigger meetings and round table talks.

U-Shape

Long tables in a U with chairs on the outside and a presenter table at the open end.

Good for Training and meetings with one presenter.

Horseshoe

Chairs in a curve facing a presenter table. No tables for the group.

Good for Small group talks and discussions.

Pod

Small groups of chairs spread around the room, with a presenter table at the front.

Good for Breakout groups and team activities.

Theatre Seating

Built in tiered seats that pull out from the wall. Oak only. Seats 150.

Good for Large talks, film showings, performances.

Banquet is the only setup photographed in Willow so far. Switch to All rooms to see the other setups.

Other layouts

We can also do Boardroom (one long table with chairs on all sides), Cabaret (small tables with chairs on one side, facing the front) and Exam (single desks in rows). There are no photos of these yet.

What to tell us when you book

  • The room and the setup name from this page.
  • How many tables and chairs you need, and which kind of table (round or long).
  • If you need a TV or projector, speakers, a microphone or extension cords.
  • If you need time to set up before the event starts.
